How it works

Every Chinese character carries one of the five elements — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water. Most of the time the answer is hiding in plain sight, in the radical.
  • 木 Wood — trees, grass, bamboo
  • 火 Fire — flame, sun, light
  • 土 Earth — mountains, stone, soil
  • 金 Metal — gold, blades, autumn
  • 水 Water — rivers, rain, winter

Five characters a day — the same five for everyone in the world. Exact match 100 · a neighbouring element that generates it 60 · a clashing element 0.
